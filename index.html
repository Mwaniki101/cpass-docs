The Problem

Skills exist everywhere. Proof of skills exists almost nowhere.

Workers

Billions of gig and platform workers have real, proven skills but no way to verify or credential them. They retrain for jobs they can already do.

Employers & Platforms

Hire blind. No reliable way to assess what a worker actually knows before onboarding. Costly training, high turnover, low trust.

Education Institutions

Cannot efficiently recognize prior learning. RPL processes are manual, expensive, and slow, often taking weeks of paperwork per candidate.

What CPASS Does

Every task a worker completes proves something. CPASS captures it.

CPASS knows which skills every job requires. When a worker completes tasks on any platform, CPASS maps that work to the skills it demanded, building a living, verified profile that compounds over time. The more you work, the more your skills are proven. The more they're proven, the more opportunities unlock.

👤 Workers

A skill profile that grows with every job completed, across platforms and verticals. See what you've proven, what you're building toward, and what new roles your skillset qualifies you for.

🏢 Employers & Platforms

Search verified talent by demonstrated skill and proficiency level. Understand what your workforce can actually do, backed by real task data, not self-reported claims.

🏫 Institutions

Receive RPL-ready candidates pre-matched to qualifications with learning outcome coverage already mapped from real work history. Assess faster, certify more.

The Engine: Why CPASS Is Different

A knowledge graph that knows what every job proves. This is the moat.

SkillGraph: tasks → skills → careers

211 skills connected by 398 edges encoding which tasks require which skills, at what level, and how they relate. Curated by domain experts. This is the inference engine.

Standards-native from day one

Every skill cross-referenced to ESCO and O*NET. Not bolted on, built in. Skills are globally portable from the moment they're recorded.

Proficiency through repetition

Do a task once and you're at foundation level. Do it 50 times across harder contexts and you're advanced. CPASS tracks frequency, complexity, and recency to infer real proficiency.

Cross-platform, cross-vertical

Work on one platform builds skills that count on another. CPASS tracks across platforms and verticals to build one unified, compounding profile.

Gap synthesis unlocks new opportunities

CPASS goes beyond showing what you have. It reveals what's missing and what new roles, qualifications, and career paths your growing skillset opens up.

Multilingual + API-first

Ingest work data in any language or local vernacular. Platforms plug in via API, send task data, and get structured skill intelligence back.

Why Now

The skill economy is arriving

  • AI is disrupting traditional work. Roles are shifting faster than credentials can keep up. Skills, not degrees, are becoming the currency
  • The gig economy is the catch net. As workers transition, gig platforms become the bridge. But without skill verification, it's a black box
  • Global work mobility is accelerating. Employers are sourcing talent across borders but have no way to verify skills across different standards and languages
  • Governments are pushing for RPL. Regulators worldwide are mandating recognition of prior learning but lack the technology infrastructure
What's Next

The flywheel

  • New verticals in days. Multi-sector architecture already mapped across construction, services, automotive, ICT, and manufacturing
  • More platforms = richer graph. Every integration adds data density, improving matching for everyone
  • Governments close the loop. They fund training institutions, set standards, and use CPASS to track how workers move through careers and into the formal economy
  • Enablement layer. Other businesses build on top of verified skill data. CPASS becomes infrastructure, not just a tool
  • Network effects compound. Workers want to be where employers look. Employers go where verified talent lives
