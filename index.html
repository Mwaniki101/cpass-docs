The Problem

Skills exist everywhere. Proof of skills exists almost nowhere.

Workers

Billions of gig and platform workers have real, proven skills but no way to verify or credential them. They retrain for jobs they can already do.

Employers & Platforms

Hire blind. No reliable way to assess what a worker actually knows before onboarding. Costly training, high turnover, low trust.

Education Institutions

Cannot efficiently recognize prior learning. RPL processes are manual, expensive, and slow — weeks of paperwork for each candidate.

What CPASS Does

API infrastructure that turns work experience into verified, portable credentials

CPASS extracts skills from how people describe their work — in any language or local vernacular — and maps them to international standards, qualification pathways, and career opportunities.

👤 Workers

See a verified skill profile, know exactly which qualifications they're ready for, and get a clear pathway to close remaining gaps.

🏢 Employers & Platforms

Search verified talent by skill and proficiency. Run skill extraction on workforce data. Get gap analysis and upskilling intelligence.

🏫 Institutions

Receive RPL-ready candidates pre-matched to qualifications with learning outcome coverage mapped. Assess faster, certify more.

The Engine — Why CPASS Is Different

A hand-curated skill graph with deep standards integration. This is the moat.

SkillGraph: Not a flat list — a knowledge graph

211 skills connected by 398 edges encoding prerequisites, complementary skills, and tool relationships. Curated by domain experts, not scraped.

Standards-native from day one

Every skill cross-referenced to ESCO and O*NET at the extraction level. Not bolted on — built in. No other product does this.

Multilingual by design

Extract skills from English or any local vernacular. Same structured, standards-linked output regardless of input language.

99.9% qualification coverage

1,630 learning outcomes mapped across 40 qualifications. Institutions plug in — no mapping work required on their side.

Gap synthesis, not just matching

We don't just say what you have. We show what's missing, how to close the gap, and the fastest pathway to a credential or role.

API-first: platforms plug in, they don't rebuild

Designed for integration. Platforms send unstructured data, get structured skill intelligence back. Simple, powerful, embeddable.

Why Now

The skill economy is arriving

  • AI is disrupting traditional work — roles are shifting faster than credentials can keep up. Skills, not degrees, are becoming the currency
  • The gig economy is the catch net — as workers transition, gig platforms become the bridge. But without skill verification, it's a black box
  • Global work mobility — employers are sourcing talent across borders, but have no way to verify skills across different standards and languages
  • Government push for RPL — regulators worldwide are mandating recognition of prior learning but lack the technology infrastructure
What's Next

The flywheel

  • New verticals in days — multi-sector architecture already mapped (construction, services, automotive, ICT, manufacturing)
  • More platforms = richer graph — every integration adds data density, improving matching for everyone
  • Governments close the loop — fund training institutions, set standards, and use CPASS to track how workers move through careers and into the formal economy
  • Enablement layer — other businesses build on verified skill data. CPASS becomes infrastructure, not just a tool
  • Network effects compound — workers want to be where employers look; employers go where verified talent lives
